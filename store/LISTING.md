## Title
Motion Lab

## Short description
Tune spring animations by feel on your phone, then export code for any platform.

## Full description
Motion Lab is a motion design tool you use with your hands. Tune a spring on a real device, with real touch and real haptics, then take the numbers and the code with you.

FEEL IT, DON'T GUESS IT
Twelve interactions, each driven by a spring you can tune with two sliders: spring drag, magnetic snap, swipe and fling, bottom sheet, pull to refresh, pinch to zoom, drag to reorder, toggle, button press, tab indicator, staggered list and like burst. Eight presets to start from, four haptic feels, and a live curve that shows settle time and overshoot as you drag.

COMPARE TWO SPRINGS
Race two springs side by side and slide between them to feel the personality shift. Or play the blind game: two springs, no names, guess which preset is which.

CAPTURE A FEEL YOU CAN SHOW
Draw a curve, sketch a motion on a mock screen with your finger, or point at a screen recording of an animation you like. Motion Lab fits a spring to it and tells you the numbers. Track two objects in one clip to get their relative timing.

TRY IT ON YOUR OWN APP
Import a screenshot of your real UI and the moving object plays over it, so you judge the feel in context.

EXPORT ANYWHERE
Everything comes from one platform-neutral spec: Jetpack Compose, SwiftUI, Flutter, React Native, Web (Framer Motion and CSS), Lottie, Figma custom-spring numbers, and design tokens as JSON, Kotlin, Swift, CSS or TypeScript. Every export includes a Reduce Motion path. Built-in checks flag sluggish, wobbly or jittery motion, and each experiment gets a simple A to F grade.

TIMELINES WITH HAPTICS
Chain experiments into a sequence, place haptic markers on it, and export the buzz pattern on its own as Android VibrationEffect, CoreHaptics or AHAP. Render the timeline to a portrait video to share.

FROM PHONE TO LAPTOP
Turn on live sync and open a link, or scan a QR code, on your laptop: the code for every platform appears in the browser and follows your sliders as you tune. A Figma plugin applies the spring to your prototype. Sync is optional, off by default, and protected by a pairing code.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud. Your experiments live on your device.
